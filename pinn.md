# Improvements and Innovation in Particle Tracking Velocimetry (PTV) -Ongoing

## Overview
Developing computational and Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) frameworks to improve the reconstruction of 2D/3D flow fields from sparse Particle Tracking Velocimetry (PTV) measurements. The research focuses on developing and validating optimization strategies for recovering underlying flow kinematics from incomplete particle trajectories, with the long-term objective of achieving high-fidelity flow-field reconstruction approaching DNS-level resolution.

## Current Work
- Conducted a literature review of existing PINN applications in PIV/PTV and sparse flow-field reconstruction to identify established methodologies, physical constraints, network formulations, and optimization strategies, and to determine opportunities for more effective utilization of PINNs in PTV. 
- Developing and validating flow-field reconstruction methodologies using synthetic datasets with known ground-truth solutions as a controlled environment for evaluating reconstruction accuracy.
- Applying affine spatial transformations, including translation, rotation, and shear, to systematically investigate the ability of computational methods to recover prescribed particle motion and underlying flow transformations.
- Comparing optimization approaches based on their accuracy, convergence characteristics, and sensitivity to transformation type and measurement conditions.
